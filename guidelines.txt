Dear All,

Your project presentations should cover the following key components:

1.⁠ ⁠Introduction: Background and motivation for the study.

2.⁠ ⁠Review of Literature: Relevant previous studies with proper citations.

3.⁠ ⁠Research Gap, Question, Hypotheses: Clearly state the research gap, your research question, hypotheses.

4.⁠ ⁠Experimental Design: Operationalization of your variables, groups, conditions, sampling method, procedure, and other relevant design aspects.

5.⁠ ⁠Limitations and Caveat: Discuss the implications and limitations of your study and how it could be extended if you find support for your hypotheses and if you don't find support for them.

Presentation Format:

•⁠ ⁠Each team will get 10 minutes for the presentation, followed by 5 minutes of Q&A with the professors.

•⁠ ⁠Each team will also get 2.5 minutes to ask a critical question to another team. Each team will have to evaluate the two other teams in their respective 1-hour slot.

Evaluation (20%):

•⁠ ⁠Background and Motivation, Research Gap – 5%

•⁠ Research Question⁠, Hypothesis and Experimental Design – 10%

•⁠ ⁠Critical Evaluation of Other Teams – 5%

•⁠ ⁠All students from that day (6 teams) must stay for the entire respective 1-hr session so that each team gets to critically evaluate two other teams.

•⁠ ⁠Actively participate in discussions and provide constructive comments on other teams' presentations.

All the best for your presentations!
